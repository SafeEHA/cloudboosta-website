Privacy notice

What we collect, and what we don't.

This covers the Cloudboosta assessments, webinar registration, enrolment and online payment, and the book-a-call and newsletter forms on this site. Cloudboosta Technology Solutions Ltd is the data controller. For anything on this page, including a request to see or delete your data, email support@cloudboosta.co.uk.

Version privacy-1.4 Last updated 21 September 2026
We never ask for keys No assessment or form asks for cloud access keys, IAM credentials, API secrets or server passwords, and you should never send them to us by email. If an engagement later needs read-only access to billing data, that is arranged separately.

What we collect

  • An assessment — your answers, and your name, company, role and work email. Optionally the country your business is in, which sets the currency on the spend question. Answers are stored with your result so a call can be prepared and the result reproduced.
  • A webinar registration — your name and email, and which webinar you registered for.
  • Enrol — your name, email, phone number, city and country, the pathway, currency and payment plan you chose, and any early-bird code.
  • A payment — if you pay online, the payment is taken by Stripe on Stripe's own page. We never see or store your card number. We keep what you paid, in which currency, when, and Stripe's reference for it.
  • Book a call — your name, email, organisation if you gave one, the topic, and your message.
  • Newsletter — your email address.

Every form carries a hidden field a person never sees; a submission that fills it in is treated as automated and discarded. We do not store your IP address with anything you send — only whether the device was a phone, tablet or desktop.

We also record which step of an assessment was on screen and whether it was finished, so we can fix questions people give up on. That uses a random identifier that lives in your browser tab and is never linked to you. There are no advertising cookies, no cross-site tracking and no third-party analytics script. The only thing kept in your browser is your progress through an assessment.

Why we are allowed to

  • Your result, and the email copy of it — the service you asked for. Your result is never conditional on agreeing to marketing.
  • A webinar you registered for — the confirmation, the joining link, the reminders before it starts and the replay afterwards are the service you asked for. Every reminder and replay email carries an unsubscribe link.
  • A payment for a place you enrolled for — the contract between us, and the accounting records the law requires us to keep.
  • A call you book, a reply to a form, the newsletter you signed up for — steps taken at your request.
  • Marketing beyond that — your consent, given by ticking the box. Never assumed.
  • Measuring where people abandon an assessment — our legitimate interest in a form that works, balanced by collecting nothing that identifies you.

Who else sees it

The companies below, each under a data-processing agreement, and nobody else. We do not sell your data and do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

  • Supabase — stores your answers, result, contact details and form submissions.
  • Netlify — serves the site and runs the scoring and form functions.
  • Resend — sends your result email, your webinar confirmation, your enrolment and payment emails, and our internal alerts.
  • MailerLite — if you register for a webinar, holds your name and email on the list for that webinar and sends its reminders and replay.
  • Stripe — if you pay online, takes the payment. Stripe receives your name, your email and what you are paying for, and collects your card details itself, under its own privacy notice.
  • Google Calendar — only if you book a strategy session.

Some of these process data outside Nigeria and the UK; transfers rely on the providers' standard contractual clauses.

Where we use AI, and where we don't

We use third-party AI models (Anthropic's Claude, and a fallback model, reached directly or through OpenRouter) for one job: reading our own webinar flyers and drafting the emails and social posts that announce a webinar. A flyer carries the speaker's name, role and photograph, which the speaker gave us to publish. The flyer and the drafts are also kept in our Google Drive. A person on our team reads and approves every word before anything is sent.

Nothing you send us through this site is given to an AI model: not your assessment answers, not a registration, not an enrolment, not a message. Assessment results are worked out by fixed arithmetic you could check by hand, and no decision about you is made by an AI.

How long we keep it

Contact details, answers and form submissions are kept for 24 months from our last contact with you, then the identifying fields are erased. What remains is an anonymous score we keep to understand how the assessment performs over time. Webinar registrations and enrolments follow the same rule; for a payment we keep the amount and date, without your name, because accounting law requires it.

You stay on a webinar's MailerLite list until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete you. A deletion request is carried out there as well as in our own database. Stripe keeps its own record of a payment for as long as the law requires it to.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, and the UK and EU GDPR where they apply to you, you can ask us to show you a copy of what we hold, correct it, delete it, stop using it for marketing, or object to or restrict other uses. Email support@cloudboosta.co.uk — you do not need to give a reason, and we respond within 30 days. Every marketing email carries an unsubscribe link that takes effect immediately.

If you are unhappy with how we handled a request, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission or to the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes to this notice

This notice is versioned, and the version in force when you submitted an assessment is stored with your consent record — so it is always clear which wording you agreed to. privacy-1.4 (21 September 2026) added webinar registration, online payment through Stripe, MailerLite, and a plain statement of where we use AI and where we don't. privacy-1.3 (26 August 2026) moved form submissions from Netlify Forms into our own database and shortened the notice. No commitment made in an earlier version has been removed.
