Enroll FAQ

Questions before you pay?

Can I pay in instalments?
Yes. Pay in full for the lowest total, or split into 2 (adds £100) or 3 (adds £200) payments across the course. Your first payment confirms your seat.
How do I pay?
Card online, or bank transfer in £, $, €, or ₦. For transfers, you'll get account details and send proof to support@cloudboosta.co.uk.
Does my payment hold my seat right away?
Yes. The moment your first payment clears, your seat is locked, even on instalments.
What if I need a custom payment plan?
Email support@cloudboosta.co.uk to arrange one. Custom plans are limited to a few per cohort.
Can I get a refund?
We offer a full refund within 7 days of your cohort start date. After the first live session, refunds are not available, but we can defer your place to the next intake if your circumstances change. Contact us before the cohort begins and we'll make it work.
When does the cohort start?
Enrollment closes 27 June 2026. Once seats fill, the next intake is months away.
