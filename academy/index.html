Got questions?

Do I need a tech background?
For Cloud Computing, no, it starts from the ground up. Advanced DevOps assumes cloud basics, and Platform Engineering and SRE assume you already have cloud and DevOps experience. Not sure? Take the 2-minute quiz.
How much time does it take?
Live classes run Saturdays from 9am, around 7 hours, for 8 weeks per pathway. Expect extra hours for practice during the week.
What if I miss a live class?
All live sessions are recorded and shared with enrolled learners within 24 hours. We recommend attending live — the cohort Q&A and discussion are where the real learning happens — but recordings mean you never fall behind.
Is it fully online?
Yes. Classes are live and online, so you can join from anywhere. You'll need a good computer and a reliable connection.
Will I get a certificate?
The pathways prepare you for industry certifications, and we provide prep materials and support. On completion, you receive a Cloudboosta certificate of achievement for your pathway.
Do you help with jobs?
Yes. Career support includes CV reviews, interview prep, LinkedIn, and referrals to our hiring network, and it continues after you graduate through our alumni community.
Can my company sponsor me, or train a team?
Yes. Talk to us about team training and corporate sponsorship.
